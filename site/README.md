## Walkthrough film

When the video is ready:

1. Save it as `assets/demo.mp4`
2. Optional captions: `assets/demo.en.vtt`
3. Reload. The film plate plays it automatically.

Until then the plate is the app icon and an honest “being cut” line. Do not drop a product screenshot in this frame.
